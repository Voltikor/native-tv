Official app artwork

The app icon pack includes developer-published artwork copied from the apps'
official Google Play listings, original Android TV app repositories, and
Google's Google TV website. Wide banner source types are recorded separately
in the manifest. For square icons, TV-specific listings are preferred; where a
separate TV listing is unavailable, the publisher's shared or standard Android
listing supplies the artwork. These are app publisher assets, not a Google icon
library. Each image keeps its publisher's colours and background and is scaled
as a complete square for the launcher.

The artwork, logos and trademarks belong to their respective app publishers.
They are used to identify the apps and are not covered by Native Tv's MIT
licence or the fallback Dashboard Icons Apache-2.0 licence. No affiliation or
endorsement is implied.

The source listings, exact image URLs, retrieval date, file hashes and fallback
coverage are recorded in native-tv/design/app-icons/official.json in the source
repository. Original source images are kept beside that record under official/.
